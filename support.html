Support

Help with SKAJ, and how to reach me.

Contact. Email feedback@skaj.app, or use Send Feedback at the bottom of Settings in the app, which fills in your app version and device for you. I read every message and reply as soon as I can.

When you report a problem, tell me what you did, what you expected and what happened. The build number is at the bottom of Settings → System.

Common questions

Where is the manual?
Inside the app: the first row in Settings, “Quick Start Guide & User Manual”. It is stored on your device and works offline.
How do I move to a new phone?
Install SKAJ on the new device and, on the first screen, choose “Replacing or adding a device?” Open SKAJ on your old device, pair the two (confirm the 4-digit code on both) and your logbook arrives. If the old device is gone, tap “Restore from a backup file” on that screen and choose your latest backup. With the same Apple ID and iCloud Keychain it opens at once; otherwise SKAJ asks for your recovery key.
How do I back up my logbook?
Automatic backup is on by default: choose a folder in iCloud Drive once (Settings → Import / Export / Backup → Backup & Restore → Fixed folder → Folder) and SKAJ saves an encrypted backup there every day. Backup SKAJ makes one whenever you want. After the first backup SKAJ shows your recovery key: save it away from your phone, in a password manager or on paper. It is also in Settings.
I lost my recovery key.
On a device that still has SKAJ and your iCloud Keychain, open Settings → Import / Export / Backup → Backup & Restore → Recovery key. If you have no such device and no recovery key, an encrypted backup cannot be opened: not by you, and not by me.
My devices will not sync.
Open SKAJ on both devices, keep them close together, and make sure Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on. In iOS Settings → SKAJ, allow Local Network. Pull down on the Dashboard or Logbook to sync now. If a device shows as not paired, pair it again under Settings → Device sync.
I lost entries after a sync.
Before a sync replaces changes that exist only on one device, SKAJ saves a safety copy. Look under Settings → Import / Export / Backup → Backup & Restore → Safety copies and restore the one from just before the sync. Then email me what happened.
Can I export my logbook?
Yes: Settings → Import / Export / Backup → Export to CSV / PDF creates a CSV file or an EASA or FAA logbook PDF, for all flights or a period you choose.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → System → Erase All Content and Reset clears the logbook in the app; deleting the app removes it from that device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. See the privacy policy.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
