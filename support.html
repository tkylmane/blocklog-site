Support

Help with BlockLog, and how to reach me.

Contact. Email BlockLogFeedback@protonmail.com, or use Send Feedback at the bottom of Settings in the app, which fills in your app version and device for you. I read every message and reply as soon as I can.

When you report a problem, tell me what you did, what you expected and what happened. The build number is at the bottom of Settings → System.

Common questions

Where is the manual?
Inside the app: the first row in Settings, “Quick Start Guide & User Manual”. It is stored on your device and works offline.
How do I move to a new phone?
Install BlockLog on the new device and, on the first screen, choose “Replacing or adding a device?” Open BlockLog on your old device, pair the two (confirm the 4-digit code on both) and your logbook arrives. If the old device is gone, restore a backup file instead: Settings → Backup → Restore from BlockLog backup.
How do I back up my logbook?
Settings → Backup → Export BlockLog backup creates a file you can save anywhere. BlockLog can also remind you once a month (Settings → Automatic Backup). Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust.
My devices will not sync.
Open BlockLog on both devices, keep them close together, and make sure Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on. In iOS Settings → BlockLog, allow Local Network. Pull down on the Dashboard or Logbook to sync now. If a device shows as not paired, pair it again under Settings → Device Sync.
I lost entries after a sync.
Before a sync replaces changes that exist only on one device, BlockLog saves a safety copy. Look under Settings → Backup → Safety copies and restore the one from just before the sync. Then email me what happened.
Can I export my logbook?
Yes: Settings → Import / Export creates a CSV file or an EASA-layout PDF, for all flights or a period you choose.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → System → Erase All Content and Reset clears the logbook in the app; deleting the app removes it from that device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. See the privacy policy.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
